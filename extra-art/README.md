# Extra art

Designs that aren't part of a regional dex: Lockatoo, the Phurbash line, the
Hyper forms and two Megas. Not shown on the site.

- `*.webp` — art (and `_shiny` variants)
- `pokedex.csv`, `megadex.csv` — their stats, abilities and dex entries, in the
  same columns as the region spreadsheets
- `abilities.js`, `moves.js` — their original abilities and signature moves

To put a design back on the site, move its row and art into a region's `data/`.
